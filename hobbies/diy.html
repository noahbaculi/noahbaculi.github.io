Do-It-Yourself

Automotive

Doing automotive work has been a productive and cost-saving hobby as well as a terrific mechanical outlet. From replacing drum brakes to repairing rust spots to adding sound-deadening, I love learning new skills and applying them to improve my quality of life. Learning about automotive mechanisms and practices has given me a glimpse into the inner workings of the machines serve as transportation for so many around the world.

Home Repairs

I thoroughly enjoy repairing items to extend their life. From replacing capacitors in electronics to sewing repairs on clothes, it can make a lot of sense both economically and ecologically. Depending on the circumstances, it can be worth the investment to resurface a table top or replace a fan bearing instead of replacing them.

Finance

As a child, I started working for a few dollars here or there, doing chores and yard work for neighbors. This income in conjunction with my scientific aptitude sparked a fascination with personal finance. Later, as I worked as professional at larger companies, my interest expanded to corporate finance as well. I enjoy combining my mathematical mindset with my data analytics experience to learn about the applications of financial instruments.

Custom Solutions

For many problems, solutions are expensive or not even available. In 2024, my girlfriend gifted me a 3D printer, which has unlocked a whole new level of product development. It's been incredible to see how far the industry has progressed since my previous experience with 3D printing during my Mechanical Engineering undergrad. Some of my favorite 'mini-projects' in this arena include:

  • Accessory mounts for IV pole
  • Foot pedal for wheelchair to replace broken piece
  • Dual exhaust upgrade for portable AC unit
  • New couch feet to fine tune sitting height
  • Simple Pull Up Bar

Woodworking

Woodworking has been a hobby of mine since high school. Being able to model and construct a physical object allows me to apply and extend my experience with physical engineering and user-centered design. Most of my projects have been functional, such as a backyard bench, or vent filter and I thoroughly enjoy being able to make something that improves an aspect of someone's life.
